# Tiruppur Textiles Master Supplier Contract Framework

## Section 1: Scope & Standard SLA Commitments
This contract establishes standard terms for yarn, chemical dyes, sewing thread, trims, and packaging materials supplied to manufacturing facilities in Tiruppur and Palladam (FAC-1, FAC-2).

### 1.1 Inbound Lead Times & Capacity Obligations
- **Primary Cotton Yarn (SUP-01 - Sri Kumaran Spinning Mills)**: Committed weekly capacity is 18,000 kg combed 30s yarn. Standard delivery lead time is 3 business days from PO dispatch. In the event of scheduled machine maintenance or boiler downtime, the supplier must provide 14 calendar days prior written notice.
- **Backup Cotton Yarn (SUP-06 - Erode Cotton Yarns)**: Secondary supplier on confirmed quote status. Quoted lead time is 4 business days with weekly emergency allocation of up to 8,000 kg at INR 278/kg.
- **Backup Cotton Yarn (SUP-07 - Salem Fine Spinners)**: Secondary backup on unconfirmed estimate. Capacity is up to 6,000 kg/week at 5 days lead time. Requires written purchase confirmation prior to batch commitment.

## Section 2: Force Majeure & Non-Performance Remedies
- In case of force majeure affecting primary suppliers exceeding 48 hours, the buyer reserves the right to reallocate pending PO quantities to pre-qualified backup suppliers without cancellation fees.
- Any supplier delay extending past promised delivery dates without advance notification triggers an automatic non-delivery notice, releasing buyer commitments and expediting substitute material sourcing.
