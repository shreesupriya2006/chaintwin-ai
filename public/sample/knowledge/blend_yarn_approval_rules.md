# Material Substitution Protocol: Blend Yarn Quality & Buyer Approval Rules

## Regulatory & Quality Constraints
T-shirt lines manufactured with combed cotton (MAT-01) can in emergency scenarios utilize a 65/35 Cotton-Polyester Blend Yarn (MAT-06) subject to technical and commercial conditions:

### 1. Eligible Product Lines
- **PRD-01 (Men's Round-Neck Basic Tee 180 GSM)**: Substitution permitted ONLY with formal written buyer consent.
- **PRD-02 (Women's V-Neck Tee 160 GSM)**: NOT PERMITTED. Premium fine-knit fabric requirement.
- **PRD-03 (Kids Printed Tee 150 GSM)**: NOT PERMITTED. Child safety skin-contact hypo-allergenic standards mandate 100% natural cotton.

### 2. Approval Procedure
- Before issuing production work orders with MAT-06, an official sample batch must be submitted to the client's quality assurance lead.
- In system recovery planning, any recovery scenario incorporating MAT-06 must mark approval status as `Assumption: Pending Buyer Approval` and increase the plan's Operational Risk score by 15 points until explicit confirmation is logged by the plant manager.
