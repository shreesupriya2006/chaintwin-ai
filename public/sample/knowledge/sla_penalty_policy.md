# Customer Delivery Commitments & SLA Penalty Policy

## Customer Tiering Structure
Customers are categorized based on contract terms, delivery commitments, and seasonal festive windows.

### Tier 1: High Priority (High SLA Stringency)
- **CUS-01 (Madras Mart Retail - Chennai)**: 2.0% daily penalty on order value for every calendar day past promised delivery date. Carries strict festive retail shelf commitments for Diwali and New Year collections.
- **CUS-04 (Hyderabad Hues Fashion - Hyderabad)**: 2.0% daily penalty on order value per delayed day. Involves high-fashion seasonal launches with contractual cancellation rights if delivery exceeds 7 days past SLA.

### Tier 2: Medium Priority (Standard SLA)
- **CUS-02 (Southern Style Stores - Bengaluru)**: 1.5% daily late penalty. Standard retail replenishment.
- **CUS-03 (Kerala Kids Corner - Kochi)**: 1.0% daily late penalty. Seasonal children's wear stock.

### Tier 3: Low Priority (Flexible Delivery)
- **CUS-05 (Coimbatore Cotton Club - Coimbatore)**: 0.5% daily late penalty. Flexible delivery window (+/- 4 days acceptable upon mutual notice).

## Penalty Calculation Mechanics
For any unfulfilled or late order:
$$\text{Penalty} = \text{Order Value (INR)} \times \text{Daily Penalty Rate} \times \text{Late Days}$$
If a delivery is rerouted or delayed due to upstream supply disruption, recovery plans must prioritize Tier 1 customers to minimize company balance-sheet damage.
