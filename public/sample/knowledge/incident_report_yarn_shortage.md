# Post-Incident Review: Q3 Yarn Spinning Mill Power Grid Interruption

## Executive Summary
On August 14, a sudden regional power grid failure disabled primary cotton yarn spinning operations at Sri Kumaran Mills for 8 days.

## Root Cause & Chain Propagation
- The outage halted spinning lines producing 30s combed yarn (MAT-01).
- Inbound POs were frozen for 10 days (8 days stoppage + 2 days restart/spin ramp-up).
- Unit 1 (Veerapandi) exhausted inventory buffers within 5 days, causing severe assembly starvation.
- 4 high-value purchase orders for Chennai retail were delayed by 6 days, incurring INR 210,000 in contractual liquidated damages.

## Lessons Learned & Corrective Guidelines
1. **Early Detection**: Delayed inbound PO confirmation must trigger immediate buffer recalculation rather than waiting for stock depletion.
2. **Speed over Spot Price**: Sourcing 5,000 kg yarn from SUP-06 (Erode Cotton Yarns) at INR 278/kg incurred a modest INR 65,000 premium, but averted INR 380,000 in retail penalties.
3. **Multi-Route Flexibility**: Road freight congestion on NH544 was mitigated in past incidents by transferring finished stock via rail link to Bengaluru DC.
