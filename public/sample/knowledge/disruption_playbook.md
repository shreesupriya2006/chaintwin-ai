# Standard Operating Procedure: Supply Chain Disruption Response Playbook

## Protocol Overview
When a tier-1 supplier experiences an operational outage or logistics embargo, managers must execute the two-level recovery evaluation protocol:

### Phase 1: Level 1 Internal Mitigation (Zero Additional Sourcing Cost)
Before expending capital on emergency spot-market purchases, evaluate internal buffers:
1. **Safety Stock Deployment**: Review factory days-of-cover. If inventory cover exceeds outage duration plus re-supply transit, maintain production line rates.
2. **Inter-Factory Stock Rebalancing**: Check secondary units (e.g. transfer MAT-01 between Veerapandi FAC-1 and Palladam FAC-2 via short-haul transport).
3. **Production Line Overtime**: Utilize standby shift capacity up to maximum daily capacity (FAC-1 max 7,500 pcs/day, FAC-2 max 5,500 pcs/day).
4. **Finished Goods Cross-Docking**: Transfer existing finished inventory between regional DCs (DC-CHN, DC-BLR, DC-CBE) to satisfy near-term retail order dates.
5. **Customer Allocation**: Prioritize Tier 1 customers (CUS-01, CUS-04) where daily penalties are highest (2.0%).

### Phase 2: Level 2 External Escalation Trigger
If Level 1 calculations show that financial losses exceed INR 500,000 or more than 2 customer orders will breach SLA, activate Level 2 levers:
1. **Engage Backup Suppliers**: Issue emergency purchase orders to pre-qualified backups (e.g., SUP-06 Erode Cotton Yarns for MAT-01).
2. **Material Substitution**: Review approved substitute material options (e.g., MAT-06 cotton-poly blend) where permitted.
